Three short training tasks with adaptive difficulty and locally saved performance. Scores reflect performance on these exercises—not IQ, general cognitive improvement, or clinical memory status.

01 · WORKING MEMORY

Adaptive backward digit span

Remember the digits and type them in reverse order after they disappear. Difficulty increases after two consecutive successes and decreases after two consecutive errors.

Memory Palace & Mnemonics Studio · V4

Build memorable connections

Learn and practice the method of loci, peg systems, chunking, and visual associations. Your custom palace and practice history are stored locally in this browser.

01 · METHOD OF LOCI

Your memory palace

Place an item at each familiar location. Imagine an exaggerated, sensory-rich interaction between the item and its location. Then recall items in route order.

02 · PEG SYSTEM

Number–rhyme peg system

Associate each numbered peg with a concrete image, then connect it to an item you want to remember.

03 · CHUNKING

Chunking workbench

Group a long sequence into meaningful units. Chunking helps when groups are already familiar; simply inserting separators does not automatically expand memory capacity.

04 · VISUAL ASSOCIATION

Imagery & linking

Turn two abstract or unrelated words into a vivid, interactive mental scene. The suggested imagery is a prompt; your own meaningful images may work better.

Mnemonic performance
